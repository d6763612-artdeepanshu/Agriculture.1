Welcome to the World of Agriculture

Agriculture is the practice of growing crops and raising animals to provide food, clothing, and other useful products.

Healthy Agriculture = Healthy Life

🌱 About Agriculture

Agriculture is one of the oldest and most important activities of human beings. It helps us produce food and raw materials. Farmers play an important role in the development of a country.

Importance of Agriculture

  • Provides food for people and animals.
  • Creates employment opportunities.
  • Provides raw materials for industries.
  • Supports the economy of a country.
  • Helps in rural development.

🌾 Major Agricultural Crops

Crop Examples Main Uses
Food Crops Wheat, Rice, Maize Used as food
Cash Crops Cotton, Sugarcane, Tea Used for trade and industries
Pulses Gram, Lentils, Peas Rich source of protein
Oilseed Crops Mustard, Groundnut, Soybean Used to produce edible oil

🚜 Modern Farming Methods

  1. Organic Farming: Uses natural fertilizers and avoids harmful chemicals.
  2. Drip Irrigation: Supplies water directly to the roots of plants.
  3. Crop Rotation: Growing different crops on the same land in different seasons.
  4. Hydroponics: Growing plants without soil using nutrient-rich water.
  5. Smart Farming: Uses technology, sensors, and data to improve farming.
